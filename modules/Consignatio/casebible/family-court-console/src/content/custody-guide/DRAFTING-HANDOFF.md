## 2. The legal spine for this posture (all VERIFIED in `verification_ledger.md` unless noted)

1. **MCL 722.27a(8):** "Parenting time **shall** be granted **in specific terms** if requested by **either party at any time**." The lead authority. Case law on its interaction with modification thresholds is **unread** — plead it as the statutory text, with the threshold argument in the alternative, never as settled threshold-free doctrine.
2. **The alternative threshold:** converting "as agreed" to a specific schedule arguably leaves the ECE untouched → ***Shade v Wright*** (verified in full) supplies the expansive standard, not *Vodvarka*; ***Pierron*** (Supreme Court, verified) sets the burden at **preponderance** where the ECE is not altered.
3. **Framing presumptions:** MCL 722.27a(1) (strong-relationship presumption; frequency/duration/type "reasonably calculated to promote a strong relationship"); **(3)** (child's **right** to parenting time absent clear-and-convincing endangerment on the record).
4. **Relief menu:** 722.27a(9)(a)–(i) — exchange logistics, specific times, notice requirements, third-party restrictions, bond; **(10)** mandatory Hague clause; communication-protocol substitution under (9)(i)/(h).
5. **Enforcement logic that justifies specificity:** MCL 552.641(2)(c) — the FOC may decline to enforce an order with no enforceable provision. "As the parties agree" is that order. Specificity now is enforceability later (M26 becomes usable only after this motion succeeds).
6. **Genesee mechanics:** LCR 2.119(A) concurrence certificate (binds pro se); **(B) proposed order attached and served with the motion**; MCR 2.119(C)(1) service 9 days by mail / 7 by delivery — and the county's posted 2019 notice is wrong (see FM-0 draft); praecipe practice PROVISIONAL.
7. **Service on a represented party:** MCR 2.107 — **quote (B) verbatim from `sources/primary/michigan-court-rules_2026-07-31.txt` before drafting the service pack** (Pass-1 item P1-5). The prior filing died on service; this cannot repeat.
