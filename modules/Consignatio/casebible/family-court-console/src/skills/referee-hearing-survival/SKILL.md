3. Don’t Do This List
Don’t explain why the relationship failed.Don’t compare whose behavior was worse.Don’t talk about alienation unless directly and narrowly asked.Don’t mention CPS unless asked directly.Don’t mention civil lawsuits, fraud, or emotional harm.Don’t argue fairness.Don’t use phrases like:

Don’t bring up the full custody dispute at a support hearing unless asked.Don’t stipulate or agree to anything on the spot unless you are 100% sure.Don’t record the hearing without explicit permission from the judge (MCR 8.115, MCR 8.109).
