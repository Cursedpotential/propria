What these checks do and do not prove

HTTP/TCP probes run from the VPS every 30 seconds while this report is open. They send no credentials and perform no writes. A login redirect or HTTP401 proves the endpoint answered, not successful sign-in. HTTP200 from an SPA does not prove its backend works; JSON health checks reject unexpected content types. Storage telemetry has its own collection interval and stale marker. Source changes, authenticated workflow checks, backups and restore verification are separate.
