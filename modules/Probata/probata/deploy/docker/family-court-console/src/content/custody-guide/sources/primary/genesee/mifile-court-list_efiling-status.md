   E-Filing Courts Directory ­ Where Can You E-File?

    The Michigan Judiciary is in the process of implementing e-filing throughout the State of Michigan. At this time,
    there are several variations of MiFILE currently being used:

         1. Courts on the Statewide MiFILE Solution (See below: Charts 1A, 1B and 1C):
                  Chart 1A ­ Circuit Courts
                  Chart 1B ­ District Courts
                  Chart 1C ­ Probate Courts

         2. Courts on the Non-Statewide Pilot Solutions (See below: Chart 2)

   Michigan Trial Court Directory

    Court location, website and contact information can be found on the One Court of Justice/Michigan Trial Courts website:
    click here for the Michigan Trial Court Directory by County.

   Looking for Access to Legal Self-Help?

    Michigan Legal Help (MLH) is an online resource that has tools and information to help you understand and manage your legal problems. You
    can access MLH at https://michiganlegalhelp.org/.

    You can also search for a legal self-help center in your community by accessing the MLH online directory at
    https://michiganlegalhelp.org/organizations-and-courts/self-help-centers.
