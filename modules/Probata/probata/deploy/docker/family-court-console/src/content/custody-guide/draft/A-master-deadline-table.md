## What this table is, and how to use it

This is **every clock in the book, in one place** — the single change-controlled home for deadlines (Acceptance Test 12). Every deadline named anywhere in the treatise carries the `⚠ TIME-CRITICAL` tier (outline line 75) **and** must appear here; no deadline may appear in prose without a corresponding row (outline line 1066). To use it: **find your triggering event** in the second column, **compute your date from the trigger date** (not from the day you read the paper), **then confirm the cite yourself** at the source before you rely on it — statutes at [legislature.mi.gov](https://www.legislature.mi.gov), rules and forms at [courts.michigan.gov](https://www.courts.michigan.gov), and Genesee local practice with the clerk or FOC.

**The standing warning:** a missed **jurisdictional** deadline (the 21-day appeal of right, MCR 7.204(A)) is usually **unrecoverable** — no judge can give the time back. A missed **discretionary** deadline (the 56-day FOC complaint window, MCL 552.641(2)(b), which says the FOC "may decline") may **not** end your right, though you should plan as if it will. The table flags which is which; when in doubt, treat a clock as running **today** and get advice (`FM.0`; free help in `FM.4` / `Module 4.7`).

**Flag legend** (from `FM-1-6-front-matter.md`):

| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **⚠ TIME-CRITICAL** | A clock is running. **Every row in this table carries this tier by definition** (outline line 75). Confirm the exact date yourself before relying on it. |
| 🔴 **ACT TODAY** | Forfeiture-grade: missing it can end the issue **permanently**. |
| ⊗ **CONFLICTED** | Sources disagree on the trigger; both are carried, no winner is picked — **use the earliest date**. |
| ℹ **PROVISIONAL / LOCAL PRACTICE** | A Genesee County local fact, sourced but **not confirmed with the court**. Confirm the specific item before you rely on it. |
| ✅ **VERIFIED** | Quoted/derived from an archived primary source with its currency stamp recorded. |
| 🛑 | Hard-stop territory (PPO/CPS/appeal/interstate) — **do not use this guide alone**; get counsel. |

> **A note on URLs.** Deep links appear only where they exist in the outline, the verification ledger, or a drafted module; MCR/MRE text otherwise rests on the archived primary (official host [courts.michigan.gov](https://www.courts.michigan.gov)); `courtrules.net` links are secondary mirrors used only where a source already used one. Where a row genuinely needs a URL not in the sources, the cell says `⚠ VERIFY — URL to confirm` rather than inventing a path.
