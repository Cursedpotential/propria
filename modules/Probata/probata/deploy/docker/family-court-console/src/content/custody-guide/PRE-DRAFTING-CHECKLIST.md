## D. Genuinely different — the recording module

**This is the one place where "just move forward" has a real cost, and it is not about affording a lawyer.**

MCL 750.539c carries up to **2 years and a $2,000 fine**. Three models produced three different legal theories about vicarious consent, and the toolkit's own audit classifies the area as **UNSETTLED**. Nobody can resolve that from a desk.

**But you do not need it resolved to get the useful answer.** All three theories converge on the same practical instruction, and that part is not in doubt:

- Do **not** record a conversation you are not part of.
- Do **not** send a child anywhere with a recorder.
- Do **not** plant a device in the other parent's home or car.
- Do **not** record the child.

**Draft that.** It is the actionable content and it is safe. What must not be drafted is any affirmative sentence saying a particular recording *is lawful* — that is where the disagreement lives, and it is the sentence that could get someone charged. The module ships with the do-not-do list and without a green light.

## E. Questions for one phone call — Genesee County Circuit Court Records

**Owner decision (Aug 9, evening): this list is a before-quoting checklist, not a prerequisite for anything.** Drafting continues regardless. Each item below is marked `ℹ PROVISIONAL` where it appears in the drafts — the mark means *check this with the court before you quote it in a filing*. Call whenever convenient; ask all of it at once; jot the answer and date next to each mark you clear.

1. Is the **7-day praecipe** requirement current, and where is it written down? (It is **not** in LCR 2.119.)
2. **Is the 2019 "Notice Regarding Motion Practice" still enforced as written?** It states the MCR 2.119(C)(4) *filing* deadline (7 days) but not the separate (C)(1) *service* periods (9 days by mail / 7 by delivery). Confirm the court expects both and how strictly each is enforced.
3. Current **motion filing fees** — general, custody/parenting-time, support-only.
4. **Who is my assigned judge, and which referee?** (The 2024 case-assignment LAO is stale against the 2025 Family Court Plan.)
5. ~~Is Genesee on **MiFILE** yet, and is the AO 2026-3 e-service pilot live?~~ **CLOSED 2026-09-07 (owner confirmation):** Genesee/7th Circuit does not use MiFILE. Paper filing remains the default. Whether the AO 2026-3 **e-service** pilot is currently live remains open and worth asking about on the same call.
6. Does the referee-objection packet's **"mailed"** trigger differ from the rule's **"served"** trigger?

Free/low-cost help that exists in Genesee County, for the places the guide flags as high-stakes:

- **Genesee County Legal Resource Center**, courthouse Room 203 `ℹ`

## G. Amendment — the attorney gate

`GUARDRAILS.md` §3 assigns the recording question and all templates to "a licensed Michigan attorney." That was written for **publishing this to strangers**, where someone else relies on it and never sees the reasoning.

**For your own case that gate does not apply the same way.** It is your matter, your risk, and your decision. What survives is narrower and does not depend on hiring anyone:

1. **No sentence claiming a specific recording is lawful** (§D) — criminal exposure, unsettled law.
2. **No template labeled filing-ready** — every one states its assumed posture and a "do not use if" list. That is a drafting standard, not a review requirement.
3. **Where an error is unrecoverable** — a missed jurisdictional appeal deadline, a CPS or criminal overlap, a UCCJEA problem — the guide says so plainly and points to the free resources in §E, rather than to a lawyer you cannot hire.
