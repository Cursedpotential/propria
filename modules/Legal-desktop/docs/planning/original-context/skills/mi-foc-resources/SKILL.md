# Michigan Friend of the Court Resources

FOC procedures, child support formula, parenting time guidelines, and Genesee County (7th Circuit) FOC specifics.

## What FOC Does

The Friend of the Court (FOC) is a division of the circuit court that handles:

1. **Custody investigations** - When directed by the judge, investigates and recommends custody arrangements
2. **Child support** - Establishes, enforces, and modifies child support orders
3. **Parenting time** - Establishes, enforces, and modifies parenting time orders
4. **Paternity** - Establishes legal father
5. **Medical support** - Ensures children have health insurance coverage
6. **Spousal support** - Establishes and enforces spousal support

## FOC Procedures

### Custody Investigation Process
1. **Referral**: Judge orders FOC to investigate (or party requests)
2. **Investigation**: FOC investigator interviews parties, children, references; visits homes
3. **Recommendation**: FOC files written recommendation with the court
4. **Objection period**: Parties have 21 days to object (MCR 3.218)
5. **Hearing**: If objection filed, matter goes to judge for de novo hearing
6. **Order**: Judge makes final custody determination

### Objecting to FOC Recommendations
- **Form**: FOC 50 (Objection to Referee's Recommended Order)
- **Deadline**: 21 days from date of recommendation
- **Effect**: Triggers de novo hearing before the judge
- **Critical**: If you don't object within 21 days, the recommendation becomes a court order
- **Strategy**: Always object if recommendation is unfavorable — you lose nothing by objecting

### Child Support Process
1. **Calculation**: FOC uses Michigan Child Support Formula Manual
2. **Key inputs**: Both parents' income, overnights, healthcare costs, childcare costs
3. **Deviation**: Courts can deviate from formula for "unjust or inappropriate" results
4. **Modification**: Can request modification if income changes by 10%+ or other material change
5. **Enforcement**: FOC enforces via income withholding, contempt, license suspension, tax intercept

### Parenting Time Enforcement
1. **Document denial**: Keep records of every denied/interfered parenting time
2. **File complaint**: Use FOC complaint process or motion
3. **Makeup time**: Courts can order makeup parenting time
4. **Contempt**: Repeated denial can result in contempt of court
5. **Custody modification**: Pattern of denial is evidence for Factor (j) — willingness to facilitate

### Domestic Violence Screening
- FOC is required to screen for domestic violence in all cases
- DV screening affects:
  - Whether mediation is appropriate (MCR 3.213 — mediation not required if DV)
  - Safety measures during exchanges
  - Supervised parenting time recommendations
  - Custody investigation approach

### Mediation
- MCR 3.213 governs mediation in custody disputes
- FOC may offer or refer to mediation services
- **Exception**: Mediation is NOT required when there is domestic violence
- Mediation is confidential — statements cannot be used in court
- If mediation fails, matter proceeds to investigation/hearing

## Agent Protocol

When handling FOC-related issues:

1. **Check**: Has a FOC recommendation been issued? Is there a deadline to object?
2. **Calculate**: Reference Child Support Formula Manual for support questions
3. **Template**: Use appropriate SCAO form (FOC series) for filings
4. **Enforce**: Document all parenting time denials using FACT method (see documentation-methods skill)
5. **Mediation exception**: If DV present, cite MCR 3.213 — mediation not required
6. **Object**: If recommendation is unfavorable, always file FOC 50 within 21 days
7. **Genesee-specific**: Check 7th Circuit local rules for any county-specific procedures
