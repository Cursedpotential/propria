## 5A. Concurrence Certificate — Genesee LCR 2.119(A)

**The rule** (VERIFIED verbatim): the certificate, "signed by the attorney of record **or
by the party in propria persona**," must be attached to or incorporated in the motion.
The heading reads "by Attorney"; the text controls, and it covers you. **Leave it off and
your motion is out of compliance with a binding local rule.**

**Two branches:** (1) concurrence requested and denied, **or** (2) reasonable and diligent
attempts to contact counsel. Because your prior requests drew no response, send a **short,
separate concurrence request by certified mail** to counsel of record — J. Marsh, 200 Main
St, Flint, MI 48502 — enclosing the proposed order and stating a response date. Then the
certificate below is true and the mailing receipt proves it. **Do it before you file; you
cannot certify an attempt you have not made.**

```
     CERTIFICATE UNDER LOCAL COURT RULE 2.119(A)

     I certify that I have made reasonable and diligent
attempts to contact counsel requesting concurrence in the
relief sought by this motion. On ____________ I mailed to
J. Marsh, attorney of record for [the other party], at 200 Main
Street, Flint, Michigan 48502, by certified mail with return
receipt requested, a written request for concurrence in the
relief sought by this motion, enclosing the proposed order and
requesting a written response by ____________. [Insert delivery
date and outcome: "The request was delivered on ____________.
No response has been received," or "Concurrence was denied by
[writing dated ____________]."]


Dated: ____________            ____________________________
                               [MOVING PARTY], in propria persona
```

> Your signature carries MCR 1.109(E)(5): it certifies you read the document, that it is
> well grounded in fact after reasonable inquiry, and that it is **not interposed for any
> improper purpose, such as to harass or cause delay.** MCR 1.109(E)(6) requires the court
> to sanction a violation. (VERIFIED.) Recite what you actually did, with dates.

## 5C. Proof of Service

**Serve both the party and the attorney.** After a final order once the appeal period has
passed, **documents must be served on the party** — MCR 2.107(B)(1)(c) (VERIFIED verbatim)
— and copies of party notices also go to the attorney of record — MCR 3.203(I)(1)
(VERIFIED). Serving **both** satisfies every reading of the rules and violates none.
Service by mail goes to the party's **last known mailing address**, which by rule is the
address of record with the Friend of the Court — MCR 3.203(A)–(B) (VERIFIED). Here that is
**PO Box 100, Flint, MI 48501**. Service by mail is complete **at mailing** — MCR
2.107(C)(3) — so the service date is the date you deposit it, not the date it arrives.
