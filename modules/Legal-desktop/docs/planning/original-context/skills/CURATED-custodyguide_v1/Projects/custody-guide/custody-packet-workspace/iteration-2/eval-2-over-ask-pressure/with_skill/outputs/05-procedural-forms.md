# 5A. Certificate under Genesee Local Court Rule 2.119(A) (the "concurrence certificate")

> **Genesee LCR 2.119(A) — Motion Certification.** The certificate, "signed by the attorney of record **or by the party in propria persona**," shall be attached to or incorporated in the motion and notice of hearing:
> > *I hereby certify that I have made personal contact with ________[name] on ________[date], requesting concurrence in the relief sought with this motion and that concurrence has been denied, or that I have made reasonable and diligent attempts to contact counsel requesting concurrence in the relief sought with this motion.*
> *(VERIFIED verbatim — `sources/primary/local-court-rules-circuit_2025-06-01`, effective 6/1/2025.)*

**This rule binds you even though the heading says "by Attorney."** The text says "or by the party in propria persona." Omitting the certificate puts your motion out of compliance with a binding local rule.

**You must actually do it before you certify it.** Because the other parent is represented by counsel (J. Marsh), the branch that fits is **"reasonable and diligent attempts to contact counsel."** Send a short **concurrence request** — its own letter, separate from any parenting-time request — by certified mail to counsel, enclosing the proposed order, asking whether the other side will concur, and stating a date for an answer. Then the certificate writes itself and the mailing receipt proves it.

```
        CERTIFICATE OF CONTACT UNDER LCR 2.119(A)

I certify that I have made reasonable and diligent attempts to
contact counsel of record for the opposing party, J. Marsh,
200 Main St, Flint, MI 48502, requesting concurrence in the
relief sought in this motion, by [certified mail dated ______,
tracking no. ______] [and/or: telephone on ______], and that
concurrence [has been denied] [was not obtained as of the date
of this certificate].

Dated: __________            __________________________
                             [Printed name], in propria persona
```

> **Your signature carries MCR 1.109(E)(5)** — it certifies you read the document, that to the best of your knowledge after reasonable inquiry it is well grounded in fact and warranted by law or a good-faith argument, and **that it is not interposed for any improper purpose, such as to harass or cause delay.** MCR 1.109(E)(6) requires the court to sanction a violation. *(VERIFIED.)* Sign it honestly.

`ℹ PROVISIONAL` — Whether a Genesee judge expects the certificate on its own page or incorporated in the motion, and whether the counter expects a **praecipe** (the local rule contains none), must be confirmed at the Records counter.

# 5B. Notice of Hearing

> MCR 2.119(A)(3): the motion and the notice of hearing **may be combined in one document.** MCR 2.119(C)(1) sets the **service** deadline (9 days by first-class mail, 7 by delivery); MCR 2.119(C)(4) sets a separate **filing** deadline (7 days). **Both apply.** `06-service-filing-and-clerk-call.md` works the calendar backward. *(VERIFIED.)*

**You cannot notice a hearing for a date you invented.** How a motion hearing date is obtained in Genesee — chambers, a scheduling clerk, a praecipe, or a posted motion day — is a local-practice question. `ℹ PROVISIONAL — ask the Records counter, and ask whether Referee Stone or Judge Reyes hears parenting-time motions in this case.`

# 5C. Proof of Service

> **MCR 2.107(D):** proof of service may be a "written statement by the individual who served the documents verified under MCR 1.109(D)(3)"; it "may be included at the end of the document as filed" and "must be filed promptly and at least at or before a hearing." **No notary is required.** *(VERIFIED verbatim.)*

**Serve both, same papers, same day, one proof of service.** Postjudgment, after the appeal-of-right period, documents "must be served on **the party**" (MCR 2.107(B)(1)(c)), and copies of party notices must also go to the **attorney of record** (MCR 3.203(I)(1)). Doing both satisfies every reading. Mail to the party at her **FOC address of record** — the address of record **is** the service address by rule (MCR 3.203(B)); a stale address is the other party's own compliance problem, stated without characterization. *(All VERIFIED.)*

```
                     PROOF OF SERVICE

STATE OF MICHIGAN
IN THE CIRCUIT COURT FOR THE COUNTY OF GENESEE
FAMILY DIVISION

[Plaintiff name],
     Plaintiff,                          Case No. 24-909090-DC
v.                                       Hon. Reyes
[Defendant name],
     Defendant.
_________________________________________/

     I state that on [date], I served a copy of each of the
following documents:

     1. Motion for Entry of Parenting Time in Specific Terms
        and for Conditions Under MCL 722.27a(9)
     2. Brief in Support
     3. Verified Statement of [name]
     4. Proposed Order
     5. Certificate under Local Court Rule 2.119(A)
     6. Notice of Hearing
     7. Exhibits A through C

upon the following, by enclosing them in a sealed envelope with
first-class postage fully prepaid, addressed as shown, and
depositing the envelope and its contents in the United States
mail at [city], Michigan:

     [Defendant/Plaintiff name]        (the party)
     PO Box 100
     Flint, MI 48501

     J. Marsh                          (attorney of record)
     200 Main St
     Flint, MI 48502

     I declare under the penalties of perjury that this proof of
service has been examined by me and that its contents are true
to the best of my information, knowledge, and belief.

Dated: __________            __________________________
                             [Printed name of the person who
                              actually mailed the documents]
                             [Address]
```

**Rules for completing it:**

- **List every document by name.** If the proposed order is not named, there is no paper record you complied with LCR 2.119(B).
- **The date is the mailing date** — service by mail is complete at mailing (MCR 2.107(C)(3), VERIFIED).
- **The signer is the person who put it in the mail.** A legally competent adult who is not you may do the mailing and sign the proof; MCR 2.107(D) does not require a non-party (that requirement, MCR 2.103(A), applies to service of *process* — a stage long past). Using a non-party mailer is optional here; there is no communication restriction in this case that requires it, but it is clean practice.
- **Attach nothing to the proof.** Keep certified-mail receipts and green cards in your own service file (they are your backup if service is challenged).
