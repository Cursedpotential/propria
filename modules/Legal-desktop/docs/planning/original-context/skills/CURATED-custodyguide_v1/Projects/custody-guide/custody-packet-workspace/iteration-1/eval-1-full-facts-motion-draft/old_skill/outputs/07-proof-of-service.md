## What to notice

- **Both the party and the attorney are served, same papers, same day, one proof of service.** This is the instruction that survives every reading of the rules: MCR 2.107(B)(1)(c) requires postjudgment service on the *party* after the appeal-of-right period, and MCR 3.203(I)(1) independently requires copies of party notices to go to the attorney of record. Doing both satisfies every reading and violates none. The Service Pack explains why in full.
- **The party's address is the Friend of the Court address of record — PO Box 100, Flint, MI 48501.** MCR 3.203(B) defines the last known mailing address as the one provided in writing to the FOC or set in the most recent order. **The address of record is the service address by rule** — you serve it even if you believe the other parent lives elsewhere. Confirm it is still the address of record with the FOC before you mail.
- **The date is the mailing date,** because service by mail is complete at the time of mailing — MCR 2.107(C)(3).
- **The signer is whoever actually put the envelopes in the mail.** Given the mail-only instruction from opposing counsel, have a competent adult who is not you do the mailing and sign this proof. Service of these documents is a court-required step, not "contact" — but do not create the argument. Keep any certified-mail receipts in your own file; do **not** attach them to the proof of service.
- **The proposed order is item 4.** If it is not named here, there is no paper record that Genesee LCR 2.119(B) was satisfied.
- **The Friend of the Court copy is not on this list** — it is handed to the clerk at filing, marked "FRIEND OF THE COURT," under MCR 3.203(G). See the Service Pack.
