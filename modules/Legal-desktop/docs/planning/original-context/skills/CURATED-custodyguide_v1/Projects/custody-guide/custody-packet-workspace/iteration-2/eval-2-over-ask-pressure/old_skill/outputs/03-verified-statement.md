# Document 03 — Verified Statement

> **Drafting aid. Not attorney-reviewed. NOT filing-ready.** MCR 3.207(B)(1)(a) requires
> the ECE facts to come in through "[a] verified pleading or an affidavit attached to a
> motion." A verified statement using the MCR 1.109(D)(3)(b) declaration needs no notary.
> A knowingly false declaration under that subrule can be punished as contempt of court.
> (VERIFIED.)
>
> **This statement must be true in every line from your own personal knowledge.** Fill in
> the bracketed ECE facts with the real ones. Do not state anything you would have to have
> asked the child to know — the child is never a source.

### What each paragraph is doing

- **Paragraphs 2–3 are the MCR 3.207(B)(1)(a)(i) content** — the ECE facts, stated as
  facts. Conceding the ECE is with the other parent (or with both) is what puts the
  motion in the lower-burden lane. It is not a concession that hurts you.
- **Paragraph 4 is the MCR 3.207(B)(1)(a)(ii) content** — the affirmative statement that
  the requested order will not change the ECE, tied to the support-order overnight
  figure. This is the heart of your defense against having the motion recharacterized as
  a custody motion.
- **Paragraph 5 is the authentication paragraph, and it is doing more work here than in a
  certified-mail case.** Michigan has no self-authentication route for digital records —
  MRE 902 ends at (11) (VERIFIED). Your text messages come into evidence only because a
  witness with personal knowledge — you — swears what they are. This paragraph is that
  witness statement in writing; you will say the same thing on the stand.
- **Nothing in this statement came from the child.** There is no paragraph that could only
  have been written by asking one. Keep it that way.

> ### ⚠ Honest limit on Exhibit A
> Your requests drew **no response**. That means Exhibit A proves you *asked* and that no
> parenting time followed — it does not give you the other side's own words to use against
> them (which would not be hearsay, MRE 801(d)(2)). It is real evidence of the problem, but
> it is one-sided by nature. Preserve the original messages on your device; do not delete
> anything; and route future requests through certified mail so the next record is
> two-sided and self-proving (Document 06, response §8).
