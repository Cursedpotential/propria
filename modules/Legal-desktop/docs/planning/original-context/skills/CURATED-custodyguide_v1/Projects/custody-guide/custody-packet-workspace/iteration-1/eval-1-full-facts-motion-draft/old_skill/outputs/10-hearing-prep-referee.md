## 1. The referee hearing is an evidence hearing

> **MCR 3.215(D)(1): "The Michigan Rules of Evidence apply to referee hearings."** *(VERIFIED.)*

This is the room where you build the record. Everything you want the judge to see later has to come in **here**, through a witness, as an exhibit. Consequences:

- **Sponsor every exhibit yourself.** Your texts, log, counsel's January 8, 2025 letter, and the support order come in through your **verified statement / testimony** (MRE 901). There is no self-authentication shortcut for text messages — MRE 902 ends at (11). See `08-exhibit-list.md`.
- **The other parent's replies are not hearsay** when you offer them — MRE 801(d)(2) (an opposing party's statement).
- **Testify to what you know.** Where you were, when, what you sent, what you received. Not why the other parent did anything — you do not know, and guessing costs more than the fact is worth.
- **The child is not a witness, source, or messenger.** Nothing you offer may come from asking the child anything. Absolute — GUARDRAILS §8.

## 2. Do not stipulate the Friend of the Court report into evidence

If an FOC report or recommendation exists or is prepared, **do not agree to admit it.**

> ***Duperon v Duperon*, 175 Mich App 77 (1989):** the FOC report "is not admissible as evidence unless both parties agree to admit it in evidence," though it "may be considered by the trial court as an aid to understanding the issues," and the court's "ultimate findings … must be based upon competent evidence adduced at the hearing."

*(VERIFIED — `verification_ledger.md` §4.2.)*

- **Stipulating converts a not-evidence document into evidence for and against you.** Unstipulated, an unfavorable report is not proof against you, and a favorable one carries none of your burden.
- **If a recommendation tracks the report** and the hearing record does not independently support it, that is an error to note and preserve — a decision that adopts FOC findings without independent hearing evidence is reversible (the *Truitt*/*Marshall* line, `verification_ledger.md` §4.2).
- ⚠ Citation-drift note if you ever quote these: *Duperon* cites the FOC duty as "MCL 552.505(d)" and the de novo hearing as "MCL 552.507(5)" — old numbering. Current: **MCL 552.505(1)(g)–(h)** and **MCL 552.507(4)**. Bracket-note any quotation.
- **Waiver-by-silence trap:** a statement of fact from the FOC report may be admitted "if no other evidence is presented concerning the fact, and the parties agree or no objection is made." `ℹ MCL 552.517d(4) — quoted via the MJI checklist; statute text not independently fetched this pass — treat as a caution, not a citation, until confirmed.` The practical point stands: **object; do not sit silent.**

## 3. Findings are required — this helps you

> **MCR 3.210(D)(1):** findings of fact and conclusions of law **are required on contested postjudgment motions to modify a final judgment or order.** *(VERIFIED — `verification_ledger.md` §2.4.)*

This binds the **judge**, not only the referee. It means a contested ruling on your motion must be explained — which is exactly what lets you object to a specific finding rather than a black box.

## 4. The clocks after the hearing — the review path

A parenting-time-only order is generally **not appealable of right** (MCR 7.202(6)(a)(iii) lists custody, physical custody, and domicile — parenting time is absent). Your review path is the **referee-objection / de novo** route, and the clocks are short. Last time a filing failed on service; this time, serve every objection correctly (Service Pack rules apply).

| Clock | Trigger | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **7 days** — object to the **accuracy or completeness** of a proposed recommended order (with a notice of hearing and an alternative proposed order) | Service of the proposed recommended order | MCR 3.215(E)(3)(d) |
| **7 days** — notice that you will use **referee-record evidence** at the judicial hearing | Before that hearing | MCR 3.215(D)(4)(c) |
| **21 days** — written objection to the referee's recommendation **plus a notice of hearing**, stating the specific findings objected to, or the recommendation stands | Service of the recommendation | MCR 3.215(E)(4) |
| **21 days** — written request for a **de novo hearing** | Recommendation **"made available to that party"** | MCL 552.507(4) |

> ### ⚠⚠ The trigger conflict — count from the earliest date
> MCL 552.507(4) counts 21 days from when the recommendation is **"made available"**; MCR 3.215(E)(4) counts 21 days from when it is **served**. These can be different days, and this project does **not** resolve which controls. `⊗ CONFLICTED — both texts verified; no case read on the interaction.`
> **The instruction is the safe move: count from the earliest date the document could have been in your hands, and file well before it.** Filing early makes the question academic — the only reliable way to win an argument you cannot resolve. 🔴 Act the day anything arrives.

## 5. What the de novo hearing is (and is not)

> **MCR 3.215(F)(2):** the court may conduct the judicial hearing by review of the referee record, **"but the court must allow the parties to present live evidence at the judicial hearing."** **(F)(2)(c)** limits only *new* evidence that was available at the referee hearing. *(VERIFIED — `verification_ledger.md` §2.3, correction C-5.)*

Teach yourself both halves: you are entitled to live evidence at the judicial hearing, but you cannot hold back evidence that was available to you at the referee hearing and spring it later. **So put your whole case in at the referee hearing.** That is the record.

- **A frivolous objection, or one for delay, can draw costs and fees** — MCR 3.215(F)(3). Object to specific findings for real reasons, not reflexively.

## 6. A short pre-hearing checklist

- [ ] Exhibits A–F assembled, each sponsorable through your verified statement / testimony; fullest available version of each digital exhibit.
- [ ] You can state, in one sentence each, what every exhibit proves.
- [ ] You will **not** stipulate any FOC report into evidence.
- [ ] Your one-sentence statement of relief is ready: specific dates, times, places; you are not asking to change custody.
- [ ] You can name the two factors you rely on — MCL 722.27a(7)(f) and (g) — and point to the exhibit for each.
- [ ] You have anticipated the response in your opening brief, because **there is no reply of right.**
- [ ] A calendar is ready for the 7-day and 21-day clocks, to run from the **earliest** possible trigger date, with service done correctly this time.

## 7. Stop and get counsel if

- The other side raises a **safety allegation**, a **PPO**, a **CPS** referral, or anything criminal. `DO NOT USE THIS GUIDE ALONE`
- Any **recording** is mentioned, by anyone, in any form. Hard stop.
- The relief in play shifts to **where the child lives**. That is a custody question with a different standard.
- The other side moves for **sanctions or fees**.
