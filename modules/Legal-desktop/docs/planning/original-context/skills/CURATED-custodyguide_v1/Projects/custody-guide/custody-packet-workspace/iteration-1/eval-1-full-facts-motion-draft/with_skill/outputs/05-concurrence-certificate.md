## The rule, and why it binds you

> **SEVENTH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT (GENESEE), Rule 2.119(A) — Motion Certification by Attorney.** "The following certificate signed by the attorney of record **or by the party in propria persona** shall be attached to or incorporated in the motion and notice of hearing filed with the clerk:
>
> *I hereby certify that I have made personal contact with ________[name] on ________[date], requesting concurrence in the relief sought with this motion and that concurrence has been denied, or that I have made reasonable and diligent attempts to contact counsel requesting concurrence in the relief sought with this motion.*"

*(VERIFIED verbatim — `verification_ledger.md` §2.5; `sources/primary/local-court-rules-circuit_2025-06-01.md`, "Updated with MSC order(s) effective on 6/1/2025".)*

**Three things to understand:**

1. **The heading says "by Attorney." The text says "or by the party in propria persona." The text controls.** You are covered by this rule. Leave the certificate off and your motion is out of compliance with a binding local rule.
2. **You must actually request concurrence before you can certify it.** Do it first. Because the other side directed you to communicate by mail (the January 8, 2025 letter), the clean move is a short **certified-mail** letter to J. Marsh that encloses the proposed order, asks whether the other party will concur, and states a date for a written answer. Then this certificate writes itself and the mailing receipt proves it.
3. **Your signature carries MCR 1.109(E)(5)** — it certifies you read the document, that it is well grounded in fact after reasonable inquiry, and **that it is not interposed for any improper purpose, such as to harass or cause unnecessary delay.** MCR 1.109(E)(6) requires the court to sanction a violation. Certify only an attempt you actually made.

## Step 2 — the certificate (sign the branch that is TRUE)

```
     CERTIFICATE UNDER LOCAL COURT RULE 2.119(A)

     I hereby certify that on ______________ [date] I requested
concurrence in the relief sought by this motion by [certified
mail to / personal contact with] J. Marsh, attorney for [the
other party], enclosing the proposed order and requesting a
written response by ______________ [date], and that

     [ ] concurrence was denied; OR
     [ ] no response was received, and I have made reasonable
         and diligent attempts to obtain concurrence.


Dated: ____________            ____________________________
                               [Your printed name]
                               In propria persona
```

- **Check exactly one box, and check the one that is true.** If counsel writes back declining, the first box is true. If no answer comes by your stated date, the second box is true.
- **Recite what you actually did, with dates.** The bare rule language would satisfy the rule, but the recital plus the certified-mail receipt is what makes the certificate provable if it is ever questioned.
