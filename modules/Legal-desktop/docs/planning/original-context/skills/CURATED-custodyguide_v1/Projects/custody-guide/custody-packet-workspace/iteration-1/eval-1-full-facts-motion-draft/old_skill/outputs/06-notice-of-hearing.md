## What to notice

- **Fill the day-of-week and the date together, and check them against a real calendar** before filing. A notice that says "Friday, [a date that is actually a Thursday]" is the kind of error opposing counsel reads aloud.
- **The motion is noticed before the referee** because parenting-time motions in a case assigned to a referee are heard by the referee (MCR 3.215). The Michigan Rules of Evidence apply at that hearing — MCR 3.215(D)(1). See `10-hearing-prep-referee.md`.
- **Both the party and the attorney are named in the "TO" block,** matching who you serve. See the Service Pack.
