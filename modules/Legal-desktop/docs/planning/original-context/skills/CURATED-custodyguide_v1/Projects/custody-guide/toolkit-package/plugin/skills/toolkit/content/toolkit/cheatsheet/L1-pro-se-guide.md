## Full foundation question-and-answer scripts
Use these as a structure, not as words to memorize. Tell the truth, use only lawfully obtained material, and adapt to the hearing question.

### Text thread
**Q:** What is Exhibit 1? **A:** “It is a copy of a text thread from my own lawfully accessed account.” **Q:** How do you recognize it? **A:** “I sent or received these messages, and I recognize the account identifier, names, sequence, dates, and content.” **Q:** Is it a fair and accurate copy? **A:** “Yes, to my knowledge; I kept the original export separately.” [source: michigan-mre-guide | pin: MRE 901; MRE 1001]

### Screenshot
**Q:** What does this show? **A:** “It is a screenshot I made from a screen I was authorized to view.” **Q:** How do you know when it was made? **A:** “The device/export record and the displayed date help me place it.” **Q:** What is its limited purpose? **A:** “It shows the message/notice as displayed, not every fact stated in it.” [source: michigan-mre-guide | pin: MRE 901; MRE 801]

### Photo
**Q:** Do you recognize Exhibit 3? **A:** “Yes. I took it on the listed date, or I personally saw the scene then.” **Q:** Is it fair and accurate? **A:** “Yes, it fairly shows what I observed at that time.” [source: michigan-mre-guide | pin: MRE 901]

### Audio recording
**Q:** What is this file? **A:** “It is an audio file received through a lawful path.” **Q:** How do you recognize voices and completeness? **A:** “I recognize the voices and can describe the file’s handling.” **Q:** What must be resolved first? **A:** “I need the court to consider legality, any local restriction, authentication, and hearsay separately.” [source: michigan-eavesdropping-and-recordings | pin: MCL 750.539a–539h]

### Co-parenting-app export
**Q:** What is Exhibit 5? **A:** “It is an export from the co-parenting account I was authorized to use.” **Q:** How do you recognize it? **A:** “It identifies the account, participants, timestamps, and sequence.” **Q:** What did you do with the original? **A:** “I retained the export and did not edit it.” [source: michigan-mre-guide | pin: MRE 901]

### School record
**Q:** How was this record obtained? **A:** “Through a lawful parent/school/court process.” **Q:** Who can explain its creation? **A:** “The appropriate custodian can explain if needed.” **Q:** What is the purpose? **A:** “Only the limited attendance/communication fact identified by the exhibit list.” [source: michigan-privilege-and-confidentiality | pin: MCL 600.2157]

### Medical record
**Q:** Is this a complete medical record? **A:** “I cannot represent that unless the custodian/certification establishes it.” **Q:** How was it obtained? **A:** “Through a lawful release or court process.” **Q:** What must be addressed? **A:** “Privilege, confidentiality, foundation, and the limited relevant purpose.” [source: michigan-privilege-and-confidentiality | pin: MCL 333.18237; MCL 330.1748]

### Police report
**Q:** Did you witness every statement in this report? **A:** “No. I can testify to what I personally observed; the report has separate foundation and hearsay questions.” [source: michigan-mre-guide | pin: MRE 602; MRE 801]

### Bank statement
**Q:** What is this exhibit? **A:** “A statement received from the financial institution/account to which I have lawful access.” **Q:** How do you recognize it? **A:** “It displays the account identifier, statement period, and transaction information.” [source: michigan-mre-guide | pin: MRE 901; MRE 803]

### Demonstrative timeline
**Q:** Who made this timeline? **A:** “I prepared it from the listed underlying exhibits.” **Q:** Is it the evidence itself? **A:** “No. It is a summary aid; the underlying items remain identified and available.” [source: michigan-mre-guide | pin: MRE 1006]

## Difficult evidence: a lawful first response
If a message appears on a child’s old tablet, a shared family-plan device, a cloud sync, or a device handed down by another adult, do not explore further. Do not use saved passwords, reset credentials, search backups, export messages, or ask the child to repeat the process. Note the device state without changing it, protect the child, and ask a Michigan lawyer about preservation, discovery, subpoena, stipulated inspection, or a qualified neutral professional. [source: michigan-eavesdropping-and-recordings | pin: MCL 750.539a–539h]

If a person gives you a screenshot, ask: who made it, what they had authority to view, whether a full lawful export exists, and whether the person can testify. Do not fill gaps by accessing the underlying account. [source: michigan-mre-guide | pin: MRE 901]

## A 20-minute hearing plan
First minute: say the narrow request. Next three minutes: give the two or three material facts with date and exhibit. Next: answer the decision-maker’s questions directly. Last minute: repeat the requested order and any next procedural date you need confirmed. This structure helps a referee or judge locate the point without treating the hearing as a full life history. [source: michigan-mcr-motion-practice | pin: MCR 2.119(F)]

**Your case is different if…** the hearing is evidentiary, a safety allegation is live, the decision-maker requests a formal offer of proof, or the hearing notice sets a different process. Follow the notice and ask for clarification rather than guessing. [source: michigan-mre-guide | pin: MRE 103]
