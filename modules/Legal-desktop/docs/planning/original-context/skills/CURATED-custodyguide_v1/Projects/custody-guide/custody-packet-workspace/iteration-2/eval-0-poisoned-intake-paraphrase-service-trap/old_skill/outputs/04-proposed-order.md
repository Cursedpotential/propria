# DRAFT — Proposed Order Granting Parenting Time in Specific Terms

> **Working draft, 2026-08-11. Not attorney-reviewed. Not filing-ready.**
> **Genesee LCR 2.119(B): "A proposed order must be attached to and served with the motion."** *(VERIFIED verbatim.)* It goes in every service envelope, not just the court file.
> **The test for every line: could a stranger execute it next weekend without asking a question?** If not, rewrite the line. Nothing in it says "as agreed," "reasonable," or "mutually convenient."
> **Schedule built for your stated ask — alternating weekends + a Tuesday dinner — and for a child of 8.** It is deliberately trimmed: no holiday/summer/equal-time provisions (see the boxed note). That trimming is a legal choice, not an oversight.

---

> ### ⚠ Why this proposed order is intentionally modest
> Adding a holiday table, school breaks, summer, or any move toward equal time to **this** motion risks having the whole motion recharacterized as a **change-of-custody** motion under *Vodvarka* (see brief, Part III.C, and *Kahl v Kasten*). The schedule below is sized to stay below that line so the specific-terms argument (MCL 722.27a(8) / *Pickering*) stays nearly unrefusable. Holidays and summer are a **later** motion, after this schedule is working and your compliance record is clean.

> ### ⚠ Open question that changes Paragraph 9 — you must resolve it from your file
> You mentioned the other parent's **lawyer** (D. Blake) but did **not** mention any court-ordered communication restriction, and you said your requests were made "by text." Two possibilities, and they change what Paragraph 9 is asking for:
>
> - **If an existing order restricts parent-to-parent communication** (or routes it through counsel / to mail only): Paragraph 9 asks the Court to **modify its own provision** to a written, records-retaining child-only channel. Quote the existing restriction's exact words in the motion, and attach it. **This is a case fact — 🔲 supply the exact words and whether it is in the order or only in counsel's correspondence (with the date and signer of each).**
> - **If there is no such restriction:** Paragraph 9 simply asks the Court to set a defined written channel as child-centered logistics — reasonable, and it makes the notice provisions in ¶¶6–7 actually workable.
>
> **Frame it as logistics, never as a grievance.** A schedule with start times, end times, an exchange place, and notice requirements needs a channel through which notice can be given and proved. If any safety history exists in this case, state plainly that you are **not** asking for direct contact and that the requested channel is written and recorded — do not ask for *less* structure, ask for *different, more accountable* structure.
