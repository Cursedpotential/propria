**A note on the texts, because it matters at the hearing.** Michigan has **no self-authentication shortcut for digital records** — MRE 902 ends at (11), and there is no analogue to the federal digital-records rules (VERIFIED — `verification_ledger.md` §2.1). So each text needs **you** as a live sponsoring witness. Practical steps:

- **Preserve everything. Delete nothing.** Screenshot each message thread showing the contact/number and the date-and-time stamps, and also back up or export the full thread.
- Print clean, complete copies for the three tabbed exhibit sets you bring to the hearing (one for the referee, one for the other side, one for you).
- Be ready to say, in one plain sentence, what each screenshot is and that it is a true copy of what is on your phone.

## Going forward — a note that also helps the motion

The order tells the parties to "communicate regarding parenting time." You have been doing that in writing. Once the court sets a written, date-stamped channel (proposed order ¶8), route future requests through it. Until then, your texts are a usable record — keep making requests in writing, keep the log current, and keep every receipt and screenshot. **Exact compliance with the communication method while you ask the court to fix it is itself your evidence** under MCL 722.27a(7)(f) (whether a parent can reasonably be expected to exercise parenting time in accordance with the order).

## What is deliberately NOT on the exhibit list, and why

- **Nothing from or about your daughter's statements.** The child's-statement hearsay exception (MRE 803A) applies "in criminal and delinquency proceedings only" by its own text (VERIFIED). She is not a witness and not a source. Absolute.
- **No recordings of any kind.** This packet does not tell you any recording is lawful; do not record the other parent, a conversation you are not part of, or the child. If any recording exists, that is a question for a lawyer before it is a question for a hearing.
- **Nothing about how the March 15, 2023 order came to be.** Every window to revisit it closed years ago; it is not relevant to what the order should say going forward.
