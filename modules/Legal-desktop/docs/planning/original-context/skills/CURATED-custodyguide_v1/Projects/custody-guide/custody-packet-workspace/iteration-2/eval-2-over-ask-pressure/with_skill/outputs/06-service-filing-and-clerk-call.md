## Who gets served — the checklist

| Who | What they get | Rule | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The other parent (the party)** | Motion, brief, verified statement, **proposed order**, concurrence certificate, notice of hearing, Exhibits A–C | MCR 2.107(B)(1)(c); MCR 3.203(A)(2), (B); LCR 2.119(B) | First-class mail to **PO Box 100, Flint, MI 48501** (FOC address of record) |

*(All VERIFIED except the PROVISIONAL rows.)* **Certified mail with return receipt** satisfies first-class mail and gives you a receipt; the receipt is for your file, not for the rule.

## The two deadlines — and why a reader who meets only one is late

- **MCR 2.119(C)(1) — SERVICE:** at least **9 days** before the hearing if served by first-class mail (7 if by delivery).
- **MCR 2.119(C)(4) — FILING:** the motion must be **filed** at least **7 days** before the hearing.

*(Both VERIFIED verbatim.)* These are **two separate obligations.** Genesee's posted 2019 "Notice Regarding Motion Practice" states the **filing** rule and is silent on the **service** rule — a parent who mails 7 days out and thinks they are on time is **two days late on service.** Meet both. And note: the filing minimum (7) is *shorter* than the mail-service minimum (9), so **file first and serve the same day, well ahead** — never file at the last legal moment or you cannot possibly serve on time.

## The fee, and the fee waiver

- A motion fee is due on filing (MCR 2.119(G)(1)). Everything filed at the same time draws **one** fee no matter how many requests are inside it (MCR 2.119(G)(2)) — so the parenting-time relief and the communication-method relief, filed together, are one fee. **Asking for a waiver is itself free.** *(VERIFIED.)* The dollar amount is a Genesee local fact. `ℹ PROVISIONAL — ask at the counter.`
- **If you cannot pay:** the SCAO **Fee Waiver Request** (commonly **MC 20** `ℹ PROVISIONAL — number not confirmed`) must **accompany** the filing (MCR 2.002(B)). If you receive **any** means-tested benefit, that is a **clerk** waiver (MCR 2.002(C)); income under **125% of the federal poverty guidelines** is a mandatory waiver, with a hardship branch above it (MCR 2.002(F)). *(VERIFIED verbatim.)*
- **⚠ The 14-day trap.** If the request is **denied**, you have **14 days from the date the clerk sends notice** to pay, or the filing is **rejected** and your hearing date goes with it (MCR 2.002(G)(1), VERIFIED). **Put that date on a calendar the day you file.**

## Where the papers go

**File at the Circuit Court Records counter — not at the Friend of the Court office.** Handing your motion to the FOC is not filing it; the FOC gets its copy *from the clerk* (MCR 3.203(G)). Formatting that gets a filing rejected (MCR 1.109(D)(1)(a); MCR 2.119(A)(2)(c), VERIFIED): 8½×11 paper, legible, English; body text **12 or 13 point**; at least **one-inch margins**; combined motion + brief no more than **20 pages** double-spaced, exclusive of attachments. The caption must carry the case number **with its year prefix and two-letter suffix** (MCR 1.109(D)(1)(b)). `ℹ PROVISIONAL — Genesee filing-counter location and hours not confirmed; MiFILE e-filing status not confirmed.`

## Keep a service file

For each mailing keep: a complete copy of the exact set mailed; the certified-mail receipt with tracking number; the return-receipt card or electronic delivery record; and a copy of the filed, date-stamped proof of service. **If service is ever challenged, this file is the answer.**
