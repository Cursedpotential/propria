## What the established-custodial-environment facts are

The established custodial environment (ECE) is a **question of fact about how the child actually lives** — with whom the child, "over an appreciable time," naturally looks to "for guidance, discipline, the necessities of life, and parental comfort" (MCL 722.27(1)(c), VERIFIED). It is not decided by the caption or by who was "right." It can exist with one parent, both, or neither.

**Fill this from your own case.** This project was not told where the child primarily resides, and does not invent it. The most likely posture, given a support order that credits you 104 overnights, is that the child primarily resides with the other parent while you exercise substantial time — but **confirm the facts and state them plainly.** The goal of this statement is to describe the current arrangement honestly and to show that specifying times does not change it.

## Rules for completing it

- **Copy the declaration sentence exactly** from MCR 1.109(D)(3)(b). Do not paraphrase it.
- **Paragraph 3 is the load-bearing one.** State the arrangement as it is. If the child primarily resides with the other parent, say so — conceding the ECE openly costs nothing and buys the lower burden. If the child in fact looks to **both** parents (a joint ECE), that is a truthful fact and it is *helpful* to the eventual 50/50 step; state it if and only if it is true.
- **Paragraph 4 keeps you honest and keeps you safe.** Describe the time you actually exercise. The proposed schedule in `04-proposed-order.md` should be written to **match** that footprint, not to expand it — that is what keeps paragraph 5 true and keeps this out of change-of-custody territory.
- **Paragraph 7 is your conduct record.** Compliance and current support are facts the court will weigh (MCL 722.27a(7)(f)). State them because they are true and because the other side will raise the subject regardless. **Never** suggest you withheld, or would withhold, either support or parenting time because of the other — they are independent obligations.
- **No adjectives, no motive, no history of the 2023 hearing.** One dated fact per sentence.
