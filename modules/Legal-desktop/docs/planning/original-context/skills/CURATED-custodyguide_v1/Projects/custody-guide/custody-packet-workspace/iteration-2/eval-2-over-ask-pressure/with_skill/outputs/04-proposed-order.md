## ⚠⚠ Read this before you fill in the schedule — the sizing control

This proposed order is written to **specify and stabilize the parenting time you already exercise** — the arrangement reflected in the support order's **104 overnights** — and to add the conditions and communication method the vague order never gave you. Done that way, this motion is, as a legal matter, very hard to refuse (MCL 722.27a(8); *Pickering*), because it does not move the child's home or change who the child looks to day to day.

**The overnight budget is your steering wheel.** Count the overnights your finished schedule produces across a year.

- **At or near ~104 overnights** (specifying the existing footprint, plus a modest defined holiday rotation and a short summer block): you stay in the lower-burden lane. This is the version this packet supports.
- **Materially above ~104, trending toward equal time (~182):** you are no longer specifying an arrangement, you are **expanding** it. A swing that large — equal alternating weeks plus half of every holiday plus six summer weeks — alters the established custodial environment and is adjudicated as a **change of custody** (clear and convincing evidence). See `07-hearing-prep-and-path-to-50-50.md`. That is the owner's goal, and it is reached in a later step, not this one.

**Every block you add past your current footprint moves the needle toward the higher-burden lane.** Add deliberately, and keep paragraph 8 of the motion (the requested order "will not change the established custodial environment") truthful.

> **Child's age:** 6. The (7)(b) nursing factor does not apply. A school-age structure (school-year weeks + defined holidays/breaks + a summer provision) is the right shape. *(VERIFIED — 722.27a(7)(b) reaches only under-6-months / under-1-year nursing.)*

### Drafting notes — NOT reader-facing

**Sizing (Phase 2 recharacterization discipline).** The example regular schedule (alternating weekends Fri 6:00 p.m.–Sun 6:00 p.m. = 52 overnights + one fixed weeknight overnight = 52) totals ~104, matching the recited figure — a specification, not an expansion. Holidays are a modest alternating rotation; summer is a short defined block, not the requested six weeks. The owner's overnight-budget instruction is the explicit control that prevents the proposed order from silently becoming the equal-time / half-of-every-holiday / six-week-summer package, which would alter the ECE and convert the motion to change-of-custody (clear-and-convincing). The owner may calibrate upward, but each block is flagged as moving toward the higher-burden lane.
