A referee recommendation/order can have a limited objection window under MCR 3.215. Record the signed/recommended-order date, service date, form revision, and deadline calculation inputs immediately, then confirm the filing path with the clerk. [source: genesee-objection-to-referee-recommendation | pin: MCR 3.215]

An objection should identify the specific factual/legal issue, the requested judicial de novo review, and the relevant record/exhibits rather than reprinting all history. What the judge re-hears and transcript treatment require current-rule review. [source: genesee-objection-to-referee-recommendation | pin: MCR 3.215]
