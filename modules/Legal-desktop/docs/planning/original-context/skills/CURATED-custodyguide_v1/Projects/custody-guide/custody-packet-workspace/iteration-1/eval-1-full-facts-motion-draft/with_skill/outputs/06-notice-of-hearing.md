## Two deadlines govern this notice — meet both

The hearing date you write here drives two separate clocks, worked in Document 8:

- **Serve** this notice and the whole motion packet **at least 9 days before the hearing by first-class mail** (7 days if by personal delivery) — MCR 2.119(C)(1).
- **File** the motion **at least 7 days before the hearing** — MCR 2.119(C)(4).

Both apply. The county's older posted notice mentions only the filing rule; a parent who mails 7 days out is two days late on service. File early and serve the same day.
