# DRAFT — Verified Statement (ECE facts)

> **Working draft, 2026-08-11. Not attorney-reviewed. Not filing-ready.**
> Purpose: satisfy **MCR 3.207(B)(1)(a)** — a verified pleading or affidavit requesting a change of parenting time must state the ECE facts. *(VERIFIED verbatim as to text; ⚠ PROVISIONAL as to whether it binds an ordinary noticed motion — include it either way; it costs a paragraph and it is exactly what the court must find under MCL 722.27(1)(c).)*
> Verify by the declaration language below — **no notary required** (MCR 1.109(D)(3)(b)). A knowingly false declaration can be contempt of court. Every sentence must be true and stated without adjectives.

---

```
STATE OF MICHIGAN
IN THE 7TH JUDICIAL CIRCUIT COURT FOR THE COUNTY OF GENESEE
FAMILY DIVISION

🔲 [Plaintiff],                     Case No. 24-777888-DC
     v.                             Hon. 🔲 [assigned judge]
🔲 [Defendant].
_________________________________________/

              VERIFIED STATEMENT OF 🔲 [YOUR NAME]

     I, 🔲 [your name], state the following of my own knowledge,
and if sworn as a witness could testify competently to it:

1.  I am the [Plaintiff / Defendant] in this case. The minor child
    is 8 years old.

2.  The child's established custodial environment is with
    🔲 [state: with whom the child primarily lives; where the child
    attends school; who provides day-to-day care]. Over an
    appreciable time the child has looked to that person for
    guidance, discipline, the necessities of life, and parental
    comfort.  [Track MCL 722.27(1)(c)'s words.]

3.  Entry of the order I request will not change the child's
    established custodial environment. The order I request does not
    change the child's primary residence, school, or day-to-day
    care. It states the dates, times, and locations of parenting
    time that the current order leaves to agreement.
    [MCR 3.207(B)(1)(a)(ii), first option: facts establishing that
    the requested order WILL NOT change the ECE.]

4.  The parenting-time provision of the order entered
    🔲 [exact date] reads: 🔲 [quote it word for word — see Motion
    ¶2; do not paraphrase]. Since that order, I have requested
    parenting time in writing on February 10, 2025, March 15, 2025,
    and April 2, 2025; no parenting time occurred on any of those
    requests.  🔲 [Attach or reference exhibits.]

     I declare under the penalties of perjury that this verified
statement has been examined by me and that its contents are true to
the best of my information, knowledge, and belief.

Dated: __________          __________________________________
                           🔲 [Printed name]
```

---

**How to complete this without stepping on a rake:**

- **Paragraph 2 must be true and concrete.** If the child primarily lives with the other parent, say so. Conceding the ECE openly costs nothing and is what puts your modest ask in the *Shade*/preponderance lane (brief, Part III.B).
- **Do not describe the other parent.** Describe where the child lives and who does the day-to-day. One fact per sentence.
- **Paragraph 4 quotes the order** — the same verbatim provision you must transcribe in Motion ¶2. If you have not pulled the order yet, you cannot finish this statement either. That is the intake gate doing its job, not an obstacle for its own sake.
