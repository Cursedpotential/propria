## What this motion is

**Postjudgment.** An order already exists in your Genesee County case (entered March 15, 2023). You are asking the court to state your parenting time in **specific terms** — days, times, and an exchange place — and to set the conditions that make those terms work, so the order can be followed and enforced without anyone having to agree to anything first. **This is not an appeal, not a reconsideration, and not a request to change custody.**

One sentence to keep at the top of your own notes: *You are asking the court to write down when parenting time happens, so a stranger holding only the order would know where your daughter is, and when.*

## Why each paragraph is written the way it is

- **Paragraph 2 quotes the order.** Everything after it is measured against those words. "As the parties agree" states no date, time, or place — that is the whole problem, and paragraph 3 says so in the court's own frame.
- **Paragraphs 4 and 6 separate two documents that must not be blurred.** The *order* says "the parties shall communicate regarding parenting time." The *mail-only* direction is in counsel's January 8, 2025 letter — **not** in the order. You attach the letter and state that fact neutrally. You do not argue about it; the relief in the proposed order (a court-set written channel) resolves it going forward.
- **Paragraph 5 is the request record, flat.** Three dates, one outcome each, no adjectives. Fill each outcome from your log exactly — "no response" where a text went unanswered, "declined, no alternative offered" where it was refused. Numbers and dates, never "always" or "refuses."
- **Paragraph 7 concedes the established custodial environment openly.** Conceding where your daughter primarily lives is not a loss — it is what puts this motion in the lower-burden lane (see the Brief, Document 3). **You must complete this from your own knowledge**; it was not supplied and must not be invented for you.
- **Paragraphs 8 and 9 are the heart of the recharacterization defense.** Alternating weekends plus one weeknight dinner is a modest schedule, well under the 104 overnights the support order already credits you. Stating that math in the motion is what keeps the other side from re-labeling this as a bid to change custody.
- **Paragraph 10 states the lead statute, exactly.** Paragraph 11 pleads the alternative in one sentence and never claims subsection (8) removes any threshold — because no one has read case law resolving that, and claiming it would be a claim you cannot back.
- **The history gets zero paragraphs.** Nothing about how the March 15, 2023 order came to be. Every window to revisit it closed years ago; the motion goes forward.
