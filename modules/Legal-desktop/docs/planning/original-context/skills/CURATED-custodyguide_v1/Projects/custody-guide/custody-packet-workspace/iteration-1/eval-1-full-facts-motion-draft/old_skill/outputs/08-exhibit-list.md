## What is not on this list, and why

- **Nothing from or about the child's statements.** MRE 803A applies "in criminal and delinquency proceedings only" by its own text. *(VERIFIED.)* The child is not a witness, not a source, and not a messenger. This is absolute.
- **No recordings of any kind.** This packet does not tell anyone that any recording is lawful. If any recording exists or is being considered, **stop and get counsel** — that is a hard-stop question outside this packet.
- **Nothing about the March 15, 2023 hearing.** It is not relevant to what the order should say going forward, and every window to revisit it closed long ago.

## Two things to do before the hearing

1. **Bring the originals or the fullest available version of each digital exhibit.** A photograph of a phone screen is sponsorable; be ready to describe exactly how you made it. If you can export the full message thread (with timestamps) rather than crop a screenshot, do that — a complete thread is harder to attack than a fragment.
2. **Do not stipulate the Friend of the Court report into evidence** if one exists or is prepared. See `10-hearing-prep-referee.md` — stipulating converts a not-evidence document into evidence for and against you.
