## 3. What you already got right (so this moves fast once the gate clears)

- **Your ask is sized correctly.** "Every other weekend + a Tuesday dinner" sits *below* the line that gets a parenting-time motion recharacterized as a custody motion. In *Kahl v Kasten* (COA, unpub. 1/15/2026) a father who asked for equal time **plus** holidays **plus** summers in one motion got his case treated as a change-of-custody motion under the tougher *Vodvarka* standard. You didn't over-ask. That's the single most common self-inflicted wound and you avoided it.
- **The overnights, counsel address, child's age, and dated requests are all in hand.** Once #1–#5 close, drafting the motion + brief + proposed order + concurrence certificate + notice of hearing + service pack is quick.

## 5. The service correction, stated plainly

I'm not going to "just serve her at her house." Here's the rule I'm following instead, and why it protects you:

- **at her FOC address of record** (MCR 3.203), which is item #4, **not** an address asserted as "where she is," **and**

Get me #4 as the address of record and the service pack is safe. That's the difference between filing once and filing twice.
