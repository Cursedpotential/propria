# Verified Statement — DRAFT

> **Work product for your own case, August 11, 2026. Not reviewed by an attorney. Not filing-ready.**
> MCR 3.207(B)(1)(a) is satisfied by "a verified pleading or an affidavit." A **verified statement** using the MCR 1.109(D)(3)(b) declaration is the simplest route and needs no notary. *(VERIFIED.)*
> This statement does two jobs: it states the established-custodial-environment facts, and it **authenticates your text messages and log** so the exhibits can come in. Michigan has no self-authentication shortcut for text messages (MRE 902 ends at (11)) — a witness with personal knowledge is how they are admitted (MRE 901). That witness is you.

## What to notice

- **Paragraph 6 is the authentication paragraph for the texts.** It is what lets the messages and any replies come in — a witness with personal knowledge saying what each document is (MRE 901). Say honestly how the copies were made. If you exported them rather than photographed them, say that instead.
- **Paragraphs 2 and 3 are the ECE facts** MCR 3.207(B)(1)(a)(i) calls for, and paragraph 4 is the (a)(ii) statement that the requested order will not change the ECE. Conceding the established custodial environment is deliberate — it puts you in the preponderance lane (see the brief, Part IV).
- **Paragraph 10 states your conduct audit as fact**, not adjective: no order violation, current on support, no missed exchange. Each of those is a plain statement you can stand behind. If any is not exactly true, cut it.
- **Nothing in this statement came from the child.** There is no paragraph that could only have been written by asking the child anything. The child is not a source, a witness, or a messenger. This is absolute — GUARDRAILS §8.
- **The declaration is copied exactly** from MCR 1.109(D)(3)(b). A knowingly false declaration under that subrule can be punished as contempt of court. Do not paraphrase it, and do not sign it if any line above is not true.
