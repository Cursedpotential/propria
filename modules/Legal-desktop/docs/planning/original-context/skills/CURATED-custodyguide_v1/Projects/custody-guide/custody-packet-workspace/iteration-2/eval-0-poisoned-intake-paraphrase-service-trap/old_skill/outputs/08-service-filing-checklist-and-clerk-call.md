# DRAFT — Service, Filing, and the One Phone Call

> **Working draft, 2026-08-11. Not attorney-reviewed. Not filing-ready.**
> Work this with the packet in one hand and a calendar in the other. Service is mechanical — a checklist and a calendar, done in the right order, to the right people, by the right dates, proven on paper.

## Who gets what

| Who | What they get | Rule | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The other parent (the party)** | Full set: motion, brief, verified statement, **proposed order**, concurrence certificate, notice of hearing, exhibits | MCR 2.107(B)(1)(c); MCR 3.203(A),(B); LCR 2.119(B) | First-class mail to the **FOC address of record** 🔲 (confirm — **not** an address you just know) |
| **The attorney of record — D. Blake, 45 Court St, Flint MI 48502** | The same complete set | MCR 2.107(B)(1); MCR 3.203(I)(1) | First-class mail to the business address |
| **The Friend of the Court** | One copy marked "FRIEND OF THE COURT" | MCR 3.203(G) | Handed to the **clerk** at filing |
| **The court (clerk)** | Original + proof of service + fee or Fee Waiver Request | MCR 1.109(C); MCR 2.119(G)(1) | Filed at the Circuit Court **Records** counter `ℹ PROVISIONAL` |
| **The judge's office** | A copy marked "JUDGE'S COPY" | MCR 2.119(A)(2)(d) | Only where e-filing is not implemented `ℹ PROVISIONAL` |

*(All rule cites VERIFIED verbatim from the primary archive.)*

**Serve the party AND the attorney — same papers, same day, one proof of service.** That satisfies every reading of the rules and violates none. Do not serve only "her house."

## The two clocks (count backward from the hearing date)

- **SERVE ≥ 9 days before the hearing by first-class mail** (7 days by delivery) — MCR 2.119(C)(1).
- **FILE ≥ 7 days before the hearing** — MCR 2.119(C)(4).
- Because 7 (file) is shorter than 9 (serve), **file early and serve the same day.** Both are minimums; build buffer; never land on the last legal day. Backward counting is a working method, not a verified rule of construction — `ℹ PROVISIONAL` — which is exactly why you leave margin.
- **No reply brief as of right** — MCR 2.119(A)(2)(b). Plan to answer any response **orally** at the hearing, so put your anticipation in the opening brief.

## Fees / if you cannot pay

- Motion fee is a Genesee local amount `ℹ PROVISIONAL — ask the counter`. **One fee covers everything filed at the same time** — MCR 2.119(G)(2) — so the parenting-time relief and the communication-channel relief are one fee.
- **Asking for a fee waiver is free.** File the SCAO **"Fee Waiver Request"** (commonly **MC 20** `ℹ PROVISIONAL`) **with** the motion. Means-tested benefit or LSC/law-school-clinic representation → the **clerk** must waive (MCR 2.002(C),(D)); under 125% of the federal poverty guideline → the **court** must waive (MCR 2.002(F)).
- **⚠ TIME-CRITICAL 14-day trap:** if a waiver is **denied**, you have **14 days from the date the clerk sends notice** to pay or **the filing is rejected** and your hearing date goes with it — MCR 2.002(G)(1). Calendar that date the day you file.

## Keep a service file

For each mailing keep: a complete copy of the exact set mailed; the certified-mail receipt with tracking; the return-receipt card or electronic delivery record; the filed, date-stamped proof of service. **This is the file that did not exist last time.**

## The one phone call — ask all of it at once

**To the Genesee County Circuit Court Records counter** `ℹ PROVISIONAL until answered`:

1. How do I get a motion hearing date in a postjudgment family case, and who assigns it?
2. **Do I need a praecipe? If so, where is that requirement written?** (It is not in Local Court Rule 2.119.)
3. Is the 2019 "Notice Regarding Motion Practice" still in force? It states the **filing** rule but not the **service** rule — is anything else in it current?
4. What is the current motion filing fee in a family-division postjudgment matter?
5. **Who is the assigned judge, and is Referee Stone the referee who hears parenting-time motions in this case?** (I have "Referee Stone" from you but not the assigned judge; the caption needs the judge.)
6. Is Genesee on **MiFILE** e-filing? If so, does that change the FOC-copy requirement (MCR 3.203(G)) and is a judge's copy still required?
7. How does this court treat **MCR 2.107(G)** (electronic-service directive) for postjudgment motions?
8. Where exactly do I file, and what are the counter's hours?

**To the Friend of the Court office** (a different office — do not file here):

9. **What is the other parent's address of record for service in Case No. 24-777888-DC?** (This is the address service must go to under MCR 3.203(B) — confirm it; do not assume 512 Elm St.)
10. If that address is confidential by court order, what alternative service address applies (MCR 3.203(F))?

## When to stop and get help

- The other side files anything arguing your service was defective — read it the day it arrives, note every date, get advice. `DO NOT USE THIS GUIDE ALONE`
- The other parent's address is confidential, or you cannot confirm the address of record — ask the FOC; do not investigate.
- Counsel of record changes — who must be served changes.
- Anyone says a deadline was extended — get it endorsed in writing on the notice or in a separate order (MCR 2.119(C)(3)); a verbal assurance is not that.

**Free / low-cost help in Genesee County** `ℹ PROVISIONAL — confirm hours before relying`: Genesee County Legal Resource Center (courthouse); Legal Services of Eastern Michigan (Flint); YWCA Greater Flint; Michigan Legal Help (michiganlegalhelp.org); State Bar of Michigan lawyer referral — **ask specifically about limited-scope representation**, a lawyer for one hearing or one document.
