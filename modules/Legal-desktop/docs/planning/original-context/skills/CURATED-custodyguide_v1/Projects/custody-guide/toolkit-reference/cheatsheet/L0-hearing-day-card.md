# L0 — Hearing Day Card

**Use the official hearing notice. Confirm county, court, case number, judge/referee, place/remote link, and start time.**

## Bring
- Hearing notice; government-issued ID if required; notebook and pen.
- One-page chronology, exhibit list, and copies only as directed.
- A short list of three asks and the source ID/pinpoint for each.
- Water, accessibility/safety plan, and a quiet way to contact support.

## Courtroom words
Address the decision-maker as “Your Honor” unless directed otherwise. Speak slowly. Say what happened, how you know, why it matters, and what narrow order you request. Do not interrupt.

## Twelve short objections
1. Relevance — “Objection, relevance.”
2. Unfair prejudice — “Objection, unfair prejudice.”
3. Hearsay — “Objection, hearsay.”
4. Lack of foundation — “Objection, foundation.”
5. Authentication — “Objection, not authenticated.”
6. Personal knowledge — “Objection, no personal knowledge.”
7. Speculation — “Objection, speculation.”
8. Privilege — “Objection, privileged.”
9. Best evidence — “Objection, best evidence.”
10. Asked and answered — “Objection, asked and answered.”
11. Compound/vague — “Objection, compound or vague.”
12. Beyond scope — “Objection, beyond scope.”

## Six foundation questions
1. What is this exhibit? 2. How do you recognize it? 3. Did you lawfully receive or create it? 4. Is this a fair and accurate copy? 5. When/how was it kept or exported? 6. What limited fact does it help show?

## If you freeze
Pause. Ask to repeat the question. Look at your one-page chronology. Say: “I need a moment to find the date.” Ask to make a brief record if necessary. Do not guess.

## Back of card
**Deadlines:** write hearing, service, response, objection, and order-review dates only after confirmation.

**Exhibits:** Ex. ___ / date ___ / witness ___ / purpose ___.

**Three asks:** 1. ___ 2. ___ 3. ___.
