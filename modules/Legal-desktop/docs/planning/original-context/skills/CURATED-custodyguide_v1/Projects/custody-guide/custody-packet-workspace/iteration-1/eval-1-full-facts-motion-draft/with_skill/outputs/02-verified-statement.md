# Document 2 — Verified Statement of the Moving Party

> **Status: working draft for your own case. NOT filing-ready.** Legal-information structure, not advice; no attorney review. This is the verified statement MCR 3.207(B)(1)(a) contemplates ("a verified pleading or an affidavit"). It uses the MCR 1.109(D)(3)(b) declaration, so it needs **no notary**. `ℹ PROVISIONAL` items must be confirmed with the court before you rely on them.

---

## What this document does

Two jobs, both required:

1. **It carries the established-custodial-environment (ECE) facts** the motion states, verified by you on personal knowledge.
2. **It authenticates your text messages** so they can come into evidence at the hearing. Michigan has **no self-authentication shortcut for digital records** — MRE 902 ends at (11), and there is no analogue to the federal digital-records rules (VERIFIED — `verification_ledger.md` §2.1). That means a live witness with knowledge — **you** — must sponsor every text. This statement is where you say, under penalty of perjury, that the texts and screenshots are what you say they are.

**Complete every bracketed line from your own knowledge.** Do not sign a sentence you cannot swear to. Where a fact was not part of what you supplied — most importantly, where your daughter primarily lives and who provides her day-to-day care — you must state it yourself; it will not be filled in for you.

## What to notice, and the traps to avoid

- **Paragraph 6 is the authentication paragraph.** It is what lets your texts come in — a witness with personal knowledge saying what each item is (MRE 901). Keep the screenshots complete: show the phone number or contact, the date/time stamps, and the full thread. Export or back them up and **delete nothing**.
- **Paragraph 3 is yours to write truthfully.** If your daughter primarily lives with the other parent, say so plainly. That concession is not a weakness here — it is the fact that keeps your burden at a preponderance (see the Brief, Document 3, Part IV). If the living arrangement is genuinely different from that, tell a lawyer before you file, because it changes the motion.
- **Nothing in this statement comes from your daughter.** She is not a source, not a witness, and not a messenger. There is no paragraph here that could only have been written by asking her something — keep it that way. This is absolute.
- **The declaration is copied exactly** from MCR 1.109(D)(3)(b). A knowingly false declaration under that subrule can be punished as contempt of court (VERIFIED — `verification_ledger.md` and `sources/primary/michigan-court-rules_2026-07-31.md`). Swear only to what is true.
