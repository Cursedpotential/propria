# Documentation Methods for Legal Proceedings

Structured documentation methods for custody cases, high-conflict communication, and evidence preservation.

## The FACT Method

Every incident should be documented using this structure:

**F - Facts:** What happened, observable behavior only
**A - Action:** What you did in response
**C - Context:** Relevant background, pattern this fits into
**T - Time:** Specific date, time, location

### FACT Example

**F:** On 3/15/24 at 6:45 PM, John arrived to pick up the children for his parenting time. I could smell alcohol on his breath from 3 feet away. His speech was slightly slurred. The children (ages 7 and 9) both said "Dad, have you been drinking?" He said "Just one beer."

**A:** I told John I was not comfortable sending the children with him when he had been drinking. I offered to let him visit at my home with me present, or to reschedule for the next day. He refused and left.

**C:** This is the 4th time in 6 months I have observed John appearing intoxicated during a parenting time exchange (previous incidents: 1/12/24, 2/3/24, 2/28/24). He has a DUI from 2022.

**T:** March 15, 2024, 6:45 PM, at my home (123 Main St)

## BIFF Communication Method

**BIFF = Brief, Informative, Friendly, Firm**

For communicating with high-conflict co-parents:

### Brief
- 2-5 sentences maximum
- Don't explain, justify, or defend
- Don't take the bait

### Informative
- Stick to facts
- Include necessary details only
- Focus on children's needs

### Friendly
- Neutral or mildly positive tone
- No sarcasm
- No emotional language

### Firm
- End the conversation
- Don't invite debate
- State your position once

### BIFF Examples

**DON'T:**
"I can't believe you're doing this again. You ALWAYS wait until the last minute and then expect me to just drop everything. I have plans too, you know. You're so selfish and you never think about anyone but yourself. The kids are going to be so disappointed that you can't even bother to show up on time. This is exactly why we're divorced."

**DO:**
"I received your request to change the pickup time from 5 PM to 7 PM on Friday. That doesn't work for our schedule. I'll have the children ready at 5 PM as originally planned per the parenting time order."

### Communication Preservation
- Screenshot texts immediately
- Forward emails to a dedicated folder
- Use a co-parenting app if ordered (creates automatic record)
- Print and organize monthly

## Pattern Documentation

Courts care about PATTERNS, not isolated incidents. Create a pattern log:

### Pattern Log Table

| Date | Incident Type | Brief Description | Evidence | Best Interest Factor |
|------|---------------|-------------------|----------|---------------------|
| 1/15/24 | Substance abuse | Appeared intoxicated at pickup | Photo, witness | (g) Mental/physical health |
| 1/22/24 | Missed parenting time | No-show, no call | Text exchange | (j) Facilitate relationship |
| 2/3/24 | Substance abuse | Children reported he "fell asleep" at 6 PM | Children's statements | (b) Capacity to provide care |
| 2/14/24 | Verbal aggression | Screamed profanities in front of children | Recording | (k) Domestic violence |
| 2/28/24 | Substance abuse | Appeared intoxicated, refused breathalyzer | Police report | (g) Mental/physical health |

### Pattern Summary Template

"In the [time period] from [start date] to [end date], [Parent] [specific behavior pattern] on [N] occasions, [missed parenting time / engaged in verbal aggression / etc.] [N] time(s). This pattern demonstrates concerns regarding Best Interest Factors [list factors]."

## Evidence Preservation Workflows

### Digital Evidence

1. **Screenshots:**
   - Include date/time stamp
   - Show full context (don't crop relevant parts)
   - Capture entire conversation thread
   - Save originals, don't edit

2. **Emails:**
   - Forward to dedicated email folder
   - Print monthly and organize in binder
   - Include full headers (shows date/time sent)

3. **Text Messages:**
   - Use screenshot apps that timestamp
   - Back up to cloud storage
   - Consider co-parenting app for automatic records

4. **Social Media:**
   - Screenshot immediately (posts can be deleted)
   - Include URL and date
   - Capture comments if relevant
   - Use web archive tools for permanence

5. **Recordings:**
   - Check state law first (one-party vs. two-party consent)
   - Label with date, time, participants
   - Store securely
   - Transcribe important portions

### Physical Evidence

1. **Photos:** Timestamp enabled, context shots (wide + close-up), store originals
2. **Documents:** School/medical/police records, organize chronologically, keep originals safe
3. **Witness Statements:** Get in writing, include contact info, note date and observations

### Organization Structure

```
Custody Evidence/
  Communications/
    2024-01-January/
    2024-02-February/
    [etc.]
  Incidents/
    Substance-Abuse/
    Missed-Parenting-Time/
    Verbal-Aggression/
    [etc.]
  Children/
    School-Records/
    Medical-Records/
    Therapy-Records/
    Statements/
  Court-Documents/
  Police-Reports/
  Pattern-Logs/
```

**Cloud Backup:** Keep everything in at least two places. Use password-protected cloud storage if the other party has device access.

## Reframing Emotions for Court

The legal system doesn't validate trauma. Translate feelings into court language:

| What You Feel | Document As |
|---------------|-------------|
| "I'm terrified of him" | "I have safety concerns based on his history of [specific behaviors]" |
| "He's a narcissist" | "He demonstrates a pattern of prioritizing his needs over the children's needs" |
| "He's gaslighting me" | "He denies events that are documented in [evidence]" |
| "I'm having a panic attack" | "I experience anxiety related to his unpredictable behavior" |
| "He's manipulating the kids" | "The children's statements reflect adult concepts inconsistent with their developmental level" |
| "I hate him" | "I have concerns about his ability to provide a safe environment based on [pattern]" |
| "He's using the kids to hurt me" | "He makes parenting decisions that appear designed to create conflict rather than serve the children's interests" |
| "I'm exhausted from fighting" | "The ongoing high-conflict dynamic is impacting the children's stability" |

**Remember:** Your feelings are valid. AND you have to speak the court's language.
