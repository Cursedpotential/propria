Court rules, local administrative orders, judge policies, forms, and deadlines change without notice — **verify everything with the Genesee County Circuit Court clerk, the Friend of the Court, the official sources cited, and, wherever possible, a licensed Michigan attorney before you file or say anything.** Missing a deadline or filing the wrong document can permanently harm your case and your relationship with your children. ## Known gaps
1. **Current local roster/policies:** the package records the 2024 domestic roster as a dated source and does not certify current assignments, motion calendars, Zoom links, exhibit mechanics, or judge policies. Confirm with the official court directory/chambers.
2. **Michigan Court/Legislature fetch blocks:** the build research tool encountered robots restrictions for several PDFs/pages. The linked official URLs are retained, but current text/revision must be opened from the official site before reliance.
3. **Forms and local packets:** county packet versions can differ from statewide SCAO forms (including FOC 68). Use the newest official form and current local instruction.
4. **Recordings and evidence acquisition:** participant-recording questions are fact-sensitive and unsettled in important respects; no script determines legality/admissibility.
5. **Deadlines:** calculator output is candidate arithmetic only. Holidays, closures, service method, court orders, and special rules can change a result.

## Escalation triggers
Seek a Michigan lawyer/qualified professional promptly for CPS/abuse/sexual-abuse claims, danger, criminal exposure, jail-risk contempt, TPR, tribal eligibility, interstate removal, appeals/missed deadlines, protected records, or official-source conflicts.
