RFAs narrow issues by asking the other party to admit, deny, or explain inability after reasonable inquiry. Track the served date and response deadline in the current rule; nonresponse can have serious admission consequences. [source: michigan-mcr-2-312-rfa | pin: MCR 2.312]

Use separate, concrete requests for date, authorship, account control, receipt, or document authenticity. Avoid compound, argumentative, harassing, or discovery-by-ambush wording. [source: michigan-mcr-2-312-rfa | pin: MCR 2.312(A)]

**Your case is different if…** an admission concerns a child’s statement, privileged matter, criminal exposure, or a record that needs a subpoena.
