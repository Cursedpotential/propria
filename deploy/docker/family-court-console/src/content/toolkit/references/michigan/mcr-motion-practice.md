A motion asks the court for a specific order. A brief explains the legal and factual grounds. Identify the relief, facts supported by affidavit/records when needed, controlling authority, hearing notice, proof of service, and a proposed order if required locally. [source: michigan-mcr-motion-practice | pin: MCR 2.119(A)(2)]

The statewide timing rule and Genesee notice must be read together with the hearing notice and any scheduling order. Do not treat a calculated date as final until clerk/calendar verification. [source: michigan-mcr-motion-practice | pin: MCR 2.119(C)(4)]

A proposed order after decision has separate entry/service mechanics; preserve proof of sending and read current court-rule text before relying on any seven-day description. [source: michigan-mcr-motion-practice | pin: MCR 2.602(B)(3)]

**Your case is different if…** an emergency, ex parte request, a protective order, a minor, an existing scheduling order, a remote hearing, or a referee recommendation is involved. Read the matching module first.
