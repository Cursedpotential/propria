# Recording Doctrine Audit

The audit treats Michigan recording/eavesdropping doctrine as unsettled. It preserves the participant-construction source, the federal AFT Michigan litigation cluster, and the Michigan Supreme Court’s non-answer to the certified question without turning them into a simple consent rule.
