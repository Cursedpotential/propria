1. **Genesee local core is strong.** Multiple Genesee CMS PDFs fetched successfully: motion-practice notice, LAO C07 2024-03 (case assignments), Joint LAO C07 2025-04J / P25 2025-02J (Family Court Plan), Joint LAO C07 2025-06J (2026 holidays), Judge Hood policies, LRC flyer, Hearsay Oct 2025, FOC form library and pro per packets, FOC Handbook 2022.

Treat domestic filings as conventional Circuit Court Records filing unless the clerk or an updated list says otherwise.
3. **Statewide primary hosts largely robots-blocked to automation.** Nearly all `courts.michigan.gov` PDFs/HTML (MCR, MRE, LCR compilation, MCSF, MJI benchbooks, SCAO FOC forms) returned `disallow_by_robots`. `legislature.mi.gov` returned `bad_robots_code`. 4. **Wrong-jurisdiction trap confirmed.** [circuit7.org](https://circuit7.org/) is the **Florida** Seventh Judicial Circuit. Genesee’s court is [7thcircuitcourt.com](https://7thcircuitcourt.com/).
5. **Bench roster is multi-source and conflict-prone.** FOC staff snippets, 2024 case-assignment LAO, 2025 Family Court Plan, Judge Hood policies PDF, and May 2026 ICLE Q&A with Judge McDowell do not form one clean current roster. **Confirm assigned judge/referee with the clerk.**

7. **MCSF 2025 is effective 1/1/2025**; Manual + current Supplement constitute the guideline (snippet of official Manual §1.01(A)). **Official Supplement deep link not isolated** this run.
8. **ICLE Michigan Family Law Benchbook** is **subscription / cite-only** ([ICLE product page](https://icle.org/books/mflb/)).

- Read master prompt Appendix B seed URLs in full.

- Second/third/fourth fetch waves covered statutes mirrors, federal eCFR/LII, MDHHS portals, Genesee CMS expansion, FOC form mirrors, caselaw.
