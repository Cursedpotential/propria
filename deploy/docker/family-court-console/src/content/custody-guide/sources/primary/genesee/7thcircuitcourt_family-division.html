Family Division

Domestic

The family division of the circuit court has exclusive jurisdiction over various family domestic matters, including divorce, custody, parenting time, child support, among others.  Personal protection orders (PPOs) are handled by the family division.

There are three judges on the family bench, with four domestic referees.

Family Division Judges

Juvenile

The family division of the circuit court has exclusive jurisdiction over juvenile matters, where the division handles juvenile delinquency cases and matters involving child abuse and neglect.

There are three judges on the family bench, with two juvenile referees
