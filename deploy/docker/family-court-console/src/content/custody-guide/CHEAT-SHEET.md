## 1. The campaign — three steps, in order, each with its authority

| Step | The ask | The law | The trap to avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Kill "as the parties agree"** | A **specific schedule** — days, times, exchange location — plus MCL 722.27a(9) terms: defined written communication channel, exchange logistics, make-up protocol | **MCL 722.27a(8)**: specific terms "shall be granted... if requested by either party at any time." ***Pickering*, 268 Mich App 1 (2005)**: "reasonable and liberal" is *plainly not* specific; refusing to consider specific terms once requested = legal error; timing is no defense (§4.3). Plead ***Shade***/***Kaeb*** threshold in the alternative (§4.1, §4.3) | **Size the ask below the ECE line.** *Kahl* (unpub. 1/15/2026): equal time + holidays + summers in one motion = recharacterized as a *custody* motion under *Vodvarka* (§4.3). Modest schedule now; growth later |
| **2. Make-up time** | Every documented denial → FOC complaint within **56 days** (MCL 552.641); make-up time under MCL 552.642 | The request record IS the case. Past un-asked months are *evidence of the problem*, not recoverable hours | Her 21-day response clock runs **from the date the FOC notice was SENT**; and the FOC route needs a **specific, enforceable term** to enforce — which is why Step 1 comes first (552.641(2)(c)) |
| **3. Toward 50/50** | After a working schedule + clean compliance record rebuilds the relationship: move for expanded/equal time | ***Bachman*** (unpub. 2015): with joint custody and an ECE with **both** parents, an equal-time request is usually *not* a custody change (§4.3 — lead) | The ECE is rebuilt by boring months of exercised time, exact compliance (722.27a(7)(f)), and zero channel violations — then the *Vodvarka* wall stops applying to you |

**Citation trap (twice over):** pre-2016 opinions cite these provisions one subsection low —
old (6)/(7)/(8) = current **(7)/(8)/(9)**. Bracket-note every quotation (§4.1, §4.3).

## 2. The clocks that can end you

| Clock | Trigger | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Serve** motion 9 days before hearing (mail) / 7 (delivery) — **AND file 7 days before** — two separate deadlines; the county's 2019 notice states only the second | Hearing date | MCR 2.119(C)(1), (C)(4) |
| Concurrence certificate + proposed order **attached and served** | Every motion | Genesee LCR 2.119(A)–(B); 7-day praecipe `ℹ PROVISIONAL` |
| **21 days** — objection to referee recommendation **+ notice of hearing**, or it becomes final | Service of recommendation | MCR 3.215(E)(4) |
| **7 days** — object to a proposed recommended order | Service of proposal | MCR 3.215(E)(3)(d) |
| **7 days** — notice to use referee-record evidence at the judicial hearing | Before that hearing | MCR 3.215(D)(4)(c) |
| **21 days** — answer an FOC make-up-time notice; **silence = you agreed** | Date notice **SENT** | MCL 552.642(2)–(3) |
| **56 days** — FOC may decline older denial complaints | Each denial | MCL 552.641(2)(b) |
| **14 days** — pay fee after waiver denial or the filing is rejected | Clerk sends notice | MCR 2.002(G)(1) |
| **21 days** — motion to amend findings | Entry | MCR 2.517(B); findings are *required* on contested postjudgment motions — MCR 3.210(D)(1) |
| **21 days** — appeal of right; **jurisdictional**; parenting-time-only orders generally NOT appealable of right | Entry | MCR 7.204(A); MCR 7.202(6)(a)(iii) |

## 3. Say it here, plead it there — mini translation table

Full card: `draft/P1-translation-card.md`. Rule: **pattern first; then argue *effect*, never
mind.** Proving the pattern doesn't unlock motive language — it replaces it.

| Never in writing | Instead |
|---|---|
| manipulation / power plays | "Between [date] and [date], agreed time was cancelled by [party] on [n] occasions: [dates]." |
| she's erasing/replacing me | "The provision has produced no parenting time since [date]." |
| she robbed me of 18 months | "From [date] to [date], no parenting time occurred; [n] written requests were made. [Exhibits.]" |
| stonewalling | "Requests dated [d1]–[d3] received no response; [d4]–[d5] were declined without an alternative." |
| narcissist / alienation / gaslighting | Delete. One dated act per sentence. Numbers over adverbs. |
| she's lying about her address | "Service was made to the address of record with the Friend of the Court (MCR 3.203)." |

## 4. The letter (Doc 1, full pattern + log in `draft/P1-interim-documentation-protocol.md`)

Short · dated · quotes the order's exact words · **two specific date/time/place options** ·
transportation offered · written answer by a named date · polite · one page · nothing about the
past, money, or the other parent. **Certified mail is the spine**; a one-line courtesy email copy
is optional with its tradeoff stated. Their replies — including every "mail only" — are
**MRE 801(d)(2) gold**. Log same-day, no adjectives. Export and bank the old emails; delete
nothing.

## 5. Absolutes

- **Exact compliance with the channel while you fight it** — it is your 722.27a(7)(f) evidence.
- **The child carries nothing, is asked nothing, is told nothing** — GUARDRAILS §8.
- **No recording green-lights.** Do not record the child, or anyone's conversation you're not in.
- **Nothing gets labeled filing-ready.** Every `ℹ PROVISIONAL` = one clerk question before use.
- Support and parenting time are separate obligations — never condition one on the other.
- Every tool here points both ways — the same statutes score *your* missed time and *your*
  channel breaches.
