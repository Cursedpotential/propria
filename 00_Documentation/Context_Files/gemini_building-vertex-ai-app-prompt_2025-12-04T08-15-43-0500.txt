You have hit on a very sophisticated database optimization topic known as **"Index Locality"** or **"Clustered Indexing."**

You are absolutely correct. Standard UUIDs (Version 4) are completely random. When you insert them into a database, the CPU has to jump around the hard drive/memory randomly to find where to put them. This causes "Page Splitting" and slows everything down as the data grows.

To fix this, but keep the "Unique" benefits, we will use **UUID Version 7 (UUIDv7)**.

- **Anticipated by CPU:** UUIDv7 starts with a **Timestamp**. This means new records are always added to the _end_ of the database file. The CPU loves this (it's sequential).
- **The "Short" Hash:** For your requirement of "6-8 characters," we will generate the full UUIDv7 for the database (safety), but we will add a **"Display ID"** property that shows just the unique suffix or a truncated hash for the GUI.
