### **What is "Fine-Tuning"?**

Taking a pre-trained AI (like Qwen) and teaching it your specific task (detecting abuse patterns).

**Analogy:**

- Pre-trained model = College graduate who speaks English
- Fine-tuning = Teaching them to be a psychologist who spots manipulation


### **What is "GPTQ 4-bit Quantization"?**

Compressing the AI model so it uses less memory.

**Analogy:**

- Normal model = 4K Ultra HD movie (12GB)
- Quantized model = Same movie in 1080p (3GB)
- Looks almost as good, runs way faster


### **What is "LoRA" (Low-Rank Adaptation)?**

A training trick that only updates 1% of the model's parameters.

**Analogy:**

- Normal training = Repainting entire house (slow, expensive)
- LoRA = Just repainting the trim (fast, cheap, same result)


### **What is "Multi-Label Classification"?**

A message can have MULTIPLE abuse tags at once.

**Example:**
"You're just high and I never said that" =

- Gaslighting (95% confidence)
- Substance weaponization (87% confidence)
- Dismissiveness (76% confidence)
