## Implemented contract

`ContextExportManifest` pins export ID/version/predecessor digest, matter, creator/purpose/audience, generator/template/redaction-profile versions, output kind, snapshot and as-lived cutoff or hindsight mode, approved route ID/map version/binding, fresh versioned destination, selected entry IDs, source IDs/versions/locators/content-hash observations/status/availability, entry record IDs/versions/claim-source relations/origin/privacy/time text/precision/basis/review/evidence-status and governed entry-availability snapshot, explicit requested raw source copies, member kinds/digests, offline member links, and unavailable/excluded source IDs. It returns a deterministic canonical SHA-256 digest for an identical manifest and route binding. Nonsemantic source/copy/file/link/selection tuples must be sorted; narrative entry tuple order is meaningful display order and is retained in the digest. Frozen dataclasses and tuple validation prevent ordinary accidental mutation of the package shape. It creates **no** canonical event, source, evidence identity, or second corpus.

The validator rejects mismatched matter/kind/route version/effective time, noncanonical or encoded traversal paths and Windows aliases/reserved components, wrong versioned destination, omitted or extra selected entries, unrequested/missing or digest-mismatched raw source copies, absent offline-link targets, duplicate IDs/paths, invalid hashes/versions/time text, future sources **or entries** in an as-lived selection, and private-strategy-labelled entries in internal/external audiences. A non-available source must be visibly excluded and **cannot be cited by an included entry or copied**. Package members are explicitly `representation` or `raw_source_copy`; transformed derivatives are not supported as raw copies. Its only release state is `prepared_unreleased`; this never means legal adoption, approval, release, filing, service, or evidence promotion. A package can represent a pre-promotion context narrative.

## Boundaries and remaining gates

This pure contract does not authenticate a principal or route approval reference; read canonical source/entry bytes or verify locator integrity; prove the supplied acquisition/realization/entry-availability clocks by governed readback; inspect narrative text for incorrectly labelled sensitive material; enforce the physical filesystem/symlink boundary; attest file bytes/rendered links; reserve a nonexisting destination atomically; verify predecessor existence/currentness; persist immutable history; perform invalidation after source change; or obtain human release authorization. The caller must do those operations against the actual approved route registry and durable source authority before an export may be labeled ready. A snapshot/version mismatch or revoked/superseded source must fail closed or remain explicitly excluded from both content and copies. Legal release, source retirement, and evidence promotion stay in their own governed lanes. A network-facing caller must bound manifest and text sizes.
