# G17 amendment — working material, retained strategy and shared mobile/legal views

Byline: Codex · GPT-6 · October 11, 2026. Planning requirements and proposed design only; no application implementation or data migration.

## Direct owner requirements

The owner requires adopted legal working drafts to be available in the legal workdesk. The intent, potential direction, legal theory or strategy conveyed by a draft must be saved even when the draft is never used. The mobile-first Family Court toolkit shares the same data with the legal workdesk through a reduced interface.

The owner also raised an intermediate in-progress state between an AI-created work and adoption, while remaining uncertain about its exact form/name. The latest owner response accepts the working-material concept. Its exact schema is not finalized, and it is not a physical taxonomy category.

## Accepted working-material concept

Keep AI-created work as the artifact's origin/type. Working material describes tentative use: keep for consideration, annotate, edit, compare, combine or leave undecided. This is an editable workflow fact or working object, not a mandatory review checkpoint, physical folder, new source identity or legal adoption.

A working version can be based on whole works, excerpts or several contributors. Preserve the exact AI output and source package; user edits and assembly create traceable working revisions. No requirement to decide eventual use at intake.

## Retain the idea independently of the draft

A potential strategy/theory/direction is a separately identified source-linked note, connected to the originating conversation, AI work and any later working or adopted drafts. It survives a draft being unused, parked, superseded or withdrawn.

Preserve the potential purpose, proposed approach, relevant issue/matter where selected, owner context and exact supporting passages. Where available, retain contrary considerations, risks, missing support and follow-up questions. Unknown fields remain explicit rather than blocking capture.

Separate intent stated by the owner from intent suggested by an assistant or inferred by an extractor. Save the latter as a tentative interpretation with attribution; the owner can correct, expand or replace it while history remains available. Retaining a legal theory does not establish its underlying factual assertions or approve its use.

One strategy can relate to many drafts; one draft can express multiple strategies. When combining strategies, preserve contributors, earlier IDs and revision relationships. Do not silently merge original sources or erase alternative approaches. The owner may retain the strategy while abandoning every current draft.

## Adoption must surface in the legal workdesk

Adoption cannot finish with only a flag in Probata. The selected exact content must resolve to a saved legal document/revision visible in the legal workdesk's working-document list, openable and editable through its existing document service.

The workdesk item links back to the original AI work, source and retained strategy notes. Ordinary working drafts remain distinct from exact-version approval, release, filing/service and evidence. A stale document projection is reported as pending or failed; do not display complete adoption before the required target readback.

## Mobile-first Family Court toolkit shares the same records

Use the shared Family Court data/API contract and common source/document identities. The phone is a reduced presentation over the same logical records and revision history, not a second dataset with independent strategy/document copies or a manual synchronization process.

Mobile essentials: find and read saved strategy/working material, add or correct context, view linked draft/current status, and open the corresponding legal document. Keep controls touch-friendly, summaries readable and source details accessible on demand. Rich formatting/advanced review may use the fuller workdesk while preserving the same document/revision family.

An authorized change from mobile must read back in the legal workdesk, and vice versa. Expected-revision checks prevent concurrent edits from silently overwriting each other. Offline or queued actions must show pending status and cannot pretend to be shared durable saves. Access checks apply equally to summary, detail and linked source requests.

The current project convergence rule names the shared SurrealDB fct/case surface for Family Court records. Reconcile each strategy/document schema and its adapter against that existing service; do not claim every strategy or office-byte store already writes there. Preserve exact document-service ownership and shared record references, with controlled projections/caches rather than competing authority.

## Processing, receipts and preservation

Go/Temporal owns extraction, materialization, repair and related processing through bounded Activities. UI reading and editing issue ordinary authorized commands; their processing steps retain idempotent operations and exact input/target revisions.

Use durable source-linked generations and the approved DerivedKnowledge envelope/typed mapping where applicable. Distinguish saved candidate interpretation, owner correction, working use, adoption, review and per-surface projection status. No source moves, historical contract rewrites, evidence promotion or new global taxonomy/ontology is implied.
