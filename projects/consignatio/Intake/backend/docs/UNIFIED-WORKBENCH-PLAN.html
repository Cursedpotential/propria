Architecture and responsibility

Platform governed intake is an external authority boundary. A Workbench review can propose intake; it does not independently promote evidence.

Bulk review and transfer

Grid, gallery, hierarchy and graph share stable selection identities. Groups are human organization; content clusters and preservation boundaries remain distinct.

Atomic units inside atomic units

Detection works on unpacked folders, archives and partial trees. It records marker evidence, confidence, completeness, containment and logical references. A preserve-whole parent governs transfer closure without hiding nested units.

Resource and failure requirements

Lazy grid windows, bounded subprocesses, durable queues and shared host/provider admission. No whole-corpus arrays, automatic OneDrive hydration, duplicate-triggered removal or silent fallback to local models. Partial extraction and stale search projections remain visible.
